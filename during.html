Navigating Your Active Project

Master the art of adaptive project management, human-centered fieldwork, and constructive feedback as you execute your project in real time.

Phase 3: Managing & Executing in the Field

Conducting research, tracking progress, and maintaining clear communication with project sponsors.

Navigate Challenges and Change

Real-world projects rarely follow a straight path. Requirements may shift, unexpected constraints may emerge, and research may challenge your initial assumptions. Treat these moments as opportunities to reassess your approach, communicate with your partner, and make informed adjustments while keeping project goals and community needs in focus.

What to Do

As you move through the project:

  • Conduct research: Use appropriate human-centered research methods to understand users, contexts, and emerging needs.
  • Track progress: Monitor milestones, responsibilities, deadlines, and changes to the project scope.
  • Communicate regularly: Keep your partner informed about progress, challenges, decisions, and potential changes.
  • Gather and respond to feedback: Seek feedback throughout the project and determine how it should inform your next steps.
  • Adapt when needed: Revisit assumptions, priorities, and project scope when new information or constraints emerge.

Questions to Consider

  • Are we still addressing the needs and goals identified with our partner?
  • What have we learned that challenges our initial assumptions?
  • Are we communicating progress and challenges clearly with our partner?
  • Has the project scope changed, and if so, why?
  • Whose feedback are we receiving, and whose perspectives might still be missing?
  • What adjustments should we make based on what we have learned?
